Buyer Risk Reduction

Why Attachment Buyers Need More Than a Product Photo

A photo cannot confirm pin size, hydraulic flow, jobsite material or packing needs. KRATOR checks machine model, mounting dimensions, hydraulic data and working condition before quotation.

  • Pin size, arm width and center distance can decide whether the attachment fits.
  • Hydraulic flow and pressure affect breaker, shear, grapple and auger selection.
  • Packing, spare parts and destination port details help reduce export order risk.
