How to Match the Right Earth Auger

Earth augers and hydraulic auger attachments are used for post hole drilling, foundation drilling, fencing, solar pile pre-drilling, tree planting, landscaping and utility work. A suitable auger should not be selected by excavator tonnage alone. Buyers should confirm operating weight, hydraulic oil flow, operating pressure, drilling diameter, drilling depth, soil type, rock or clay condition, auger bit diameter, auger bit length, extension requirement and bracket fitment before quotation.

FAQ

Earth Auger FAQ

Common questions about matching hydraulic earth augers for excavators and drilling projects.

How do I choose an earth auger for my excavator?

Start with excavator operating weight, hydraulic oil flow, operating pressure, required hole diameter, drilling depth and soil condition.

What information is needed before quotation?

Please send excavator brand and model, operating weight, oil flow, pressure, required hole diameter, drilling depth, soil type, quantity and destination.

What hole diameter can an excavator earth auger drill?

The current auger diameter range covers 100-1200 mm depending on auger drive model and bit selection.

Can it drill in hard soil or clay?

Earth auger performance depends on hydraulic data, bit type and ground condition. For hard soil or clay, send project details for matching support.

Can KRATOR supply different auger bit sizes?

Yes. Auger bit diameter can be selected according to hole size, soil condition and excavator capacity.

Can extension rods be supplied?

Extension options can be supplied when deeper drilling is required. Please provide target drilling depth before quotation.

What excavator information should I provide?

Send excavator model, operating weight, hydraulic flow, operating pressure and bracket photos if available.

How fast can I get a quotation?

KRATOR usually replies within 12 hours after receiving machine and drilling project information.
