How to Match the Right Excavator مدك صفيحة

Excavator plate compactors are used for trench compaction, slope compaction, backfill, road construction, pipeline work and foundation compaction. A suitable hydraulic compactor should not be selected by excavator tonnage alone. Buyers should confirm excavator operating weight, hydraulic oil flow, operating pressure, plate size, compaction force, vibration frequency, soil type, trench width, slope angle and real working condition before quotation.

FAQ

Excavator مدك صفيحة FAQ

Common questions about matching hydraulic compactors for excavators and compaction projects.

How do I choose an excavator plate compactor?

Start with excavator operating weight, hydraulic flow, operating pressure, soil type, trench width and required plate size.

What information is needed before quotation?

Please send excavator brand and model, operating weight, oil flow, pressure, soil condition, working condition, quantity and destination.

What hydraulic flow is required for a plate compactor?

Hydraulic flow depends on model. The current range covers 25-45 L/min up to 120-170 L/min according to the technical table.

Can it be used for trench and slope compaction?

Yes. Hydraulic plate compactors are commonly used for trench backfill, slope compaction, road shoulder and foundation work.

What is the difference between a plate compactor and a roller compactor?

A plate compactor uses vibration through a flat plate for localized compaction, while a roller compactor is usually selected for continuous surface compaction.

Can KRATOR customize bracket size or plate size?

KRATOR can support bracket matching and plate size selection according to the excavator and project requirements.

What excavator information should I provide?

Send excavator model, operating weight, oil flow, operating pressure and photos of the arm or bracket if available.

How fast can I get a quotation?

KRATOR usually replies within 12 hours after receiving machine and jobsite information.
