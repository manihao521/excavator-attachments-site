FAQ

Frost Ripper FAQ

Common questions about model matching, quotation and export support.

How do I choose frost ripper?

Start with excavator model, operating weight, mounting dimensions and working condition, then confirm the final configuration with KRATOR.

What information is needed before quotation?

Please send excavator brand, model, operating weight, pin diameter, arm width, center distance, quantity and destination country or port.

Can KRATOR customize the bracket?

Yes. Bracket, pin dimensions, color, logo and packing can be matched according to machine data and order requirements.

Can it fit different excavator brands?

KRATOR can match common excavator brands after receiving machine model and mounting dimensions.

Do you support OEM or dealer orders?

OEM color, logo, packing and batch order support are available for distributors and project buyers.

How fast can I get a quotation?

KRATOR usually replies within 12 hours after receiving complete machine and jobsite information.
