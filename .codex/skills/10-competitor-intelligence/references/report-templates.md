## Product Category Comparison

Record:

- Category and subtype URLs
- Model naming and carrier ranges
- Visible specification fields
- Selection, application, maintenance, and spare-parts coverage
- Product-to-guide and guide-to-product links
- Quote information requested
- KRATOR facts needed before matching or exceeding useful depth

Do not copy tables. Design an original KRATOR specification framework from verified KRATOR data.

## GEO Entity Comparison

Compare:

- Organization and brand consistency
- About and factory evidence
- Product entity completeness
- Author and publisher identity
- Answer-ready definitions and decision tables
- FAQ visible-content parity
- Internal semantic relationships
- Dated, citable first-party technical material

Separate schema syntax from real-world authority. More markup is not automatically stronger authority.

## Conversion Path Comparison

Map each public path without submitting data:

`Landing page -> product understanding -> fit confirmation -> trust proof -> quote or contact`

Record CTA wording, placement, required fields, WhatsApp availability, dealer path, friction, confirmation behavior visible before submission, and mobile considerations. Convert findings into KRATOR-specific tests; do not reproduce a competitor interface.
