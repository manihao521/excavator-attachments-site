# KRATOR Content Cluster Builder

## Prepare

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/seo-strategy.md`, `docs/geo-ai-optimization.md`, the sitemap, and relevant source pages. Inventory existing KRATOR URLs before proposing a new URL. Default to planning only; do not create or edit website pages unless explicitly instructed after the plan is approved.

Use professional excavator attachment terminology. Never invent specifications, certifications, customers, production numbers, export volumes, reviews, factory facts, case-study outcomes, search volumes, or ranking claims. Record unavailable facts as required inputs.

## Supported Clusters

- Product knowledge
- Buying guides
- Comparison articles
- Maintenance and troubleshooting
- Applications
- OEM and private label manufacturing
- Factory and quality control
- Dealer and distributor resources
- Country and regional landing pages
- Case studies
- FAQ knowledge clusters

Country pages require distinct market evidence, local buyer intent, and useful regional information. Do not recommend doorway pages. Case studies require real project evidence and approval for every identifiable detail.

## Workflow

1. Define one pillar topic, product family, target buyer, market, and business objective.
2. Separate commercial, transactional, informational, and comparison intent. Assign one primary intent owner per URL.
3. Inventory existing pages and proposed pages. Prefer improving an existing URL when it can fully own the intent.
4. Generate supporting topics that answer different buyer decisions, not reworded versions of the pillar.
5. Map every topic to an existing or proposed canonical URL, page type, primary keyword, secondary terms, buyer questions, FAQ topics, schema recommendation, CTA, and related products.
6. Design bidirectional links: pillar to support, support to pillar, blog to product, product to guide, and relevant links to About, Factory, Contact, or quote paths.
7. Run the cannibalization gate before recommending publication.
8. Score and prioritize each page using the rubric below.
9. List required engineering data, factory proof, images, market evidence, and subject-matter review.
10. Return the required A-J report. Do not draft all pages unless the user separately approves content production.

## Cannibalization Gate

For every proposed page, compare primary intent, product entity, buyer stage, decision question, title concept, and target URL against the repository.

- Merge when two pages would answer the same primary question for the same buyer.
- Differentiate when the decision scope, application, subtype, or buyer stage is genuinely distinct.
- Consolidate or redirect only as a recommendation until implementation is explicitly approved.
- Flag plural/singular, synonym, subtype, country, and comparison variants that could compete with an existing page.
- Do not create a blog article that should be a section on a product or pillar page.

## Priority Score

Score each factor from 1 to 5 and explain the evidence:

- Inquiry potential: 30%
- Product relevance: 25%
- Topical authority value: 20%
- GEO and AI citation value: 15%
- Ranking feasibility: 10%, where a more achievable topic scores higher

Do not claim precise traffic, keyword volume, or difficulty without a cited data source. Use qualitative confidence when live keyword data is unavailable.

## Internal-Link Rules

- Use descriptive anchors and real canonical routes.
- Link only when the destination helps the current buyer decision.
- Ensure every new support page has at least one planned inbound link and one path to a product or qualified inquiry.
- Link product pages back to guides only when the guide is directly relevant.
- Use About and Factory for supplier validation, not as generic links on every paragraph.
- Validate proposed URLs against source and fresh `dist/` before implementation.

## Required Output

A. Cluster summary

B. Pillar page

C. Supporting pages

D. Keyword and intent map

E. Internal-link map

F. Cannibalization risks

G. Publishing priority

H. Required factual materials

I. CTA strategy

J. GEO and AI-search opportunities

Use tables where they make URL ownership, intent, links, and priority easier to audit. Clearly label existing, improve, proposed, merge, and do-not-create decisions.
