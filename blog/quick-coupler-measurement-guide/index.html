How to Measure Pin Size for an Excavator Quick Coupler

Accurate pin diameter, arm width, center distance and bucket ear width help KRATOR match the right quick coupler and reduce wrong bracket risk.

6 min readKRATOR Technical TeamUpdated 2026

Quick Answer

For quick coupler matching, the most important dimensions are pin diameter, arm width, center distance and bucket ear width. Clear photos and a simple sketch help avoid wrong bracket matching.

Quick Coupler Measurement Points

Measure the key dimensions before ordering a quick coupler. A simple drawing with clear photos helps reduce wrong matching risk.

A: Pin DiameterB: Arm WidthC: Center DistanceD: Bucket Ear Width

Key Measurement Factors

Pin diameter

Measure the pin or pin hole diameter in millimeters with a caliper.

Arm width

Measure the inside width of the excavator arm, not the outside width.

Center distance

Measure from the center of one pin hole to the center of the other pin hole.

Bucket ear width

Confirm the ear spacing on the bucket or attachment side.

Machine model

Machine plate photos help confirm standard dimensions when drawings are unavailable.

Locking type

Manual, hydraulic, tilting and rotating couplers use different structures.

Why Accurate Coupler Measurement Matters

Wrong pin size or arm width can cause installation failure, excessive clearance, unsafe locking or delivery delays. Even when the excavator model is known, previous repairs, non-standard buckets or regional machine variants can change dimensions. Accurate measurements reduce rework and help KRATOR prepare a coupler that fits the carrier and attachments.

How to Measure Pin Diameter

Use a caliper to measure the existing pin diameter or the pin hole diameter. Record the value in millimeters. If the pin is worn, measure several points and send photos so the technical team can judge the correct nominal size.

How to Measure Arm Width

Measure the inside width between the excavator arm plates. Do not measure the outside width. The coupler must fit between the arm plates with correct clearance, so this dimension is critical for bracket machining.

How to Measure Center Distance

Measure the distance from the center of the front pin hole to the center of the rear pin hole. A simple drawing with A, B, C and D dimensions is enough if photos are also provided.

Photos Buyers Should Send

Send the machine plate, arm side view, linkage photo, bucket ear photo, existing pin photo and a simple sketch with dimensions. These images help confirm whether the coupler should match the arm, bucket or both.

Final Check Before Ordering

Before confirming the order, check excavator model, operating weight, pin diameter, arm width, center distance, bucket ear width, hydraulic line requirement and whether the coupler must fit multiple buckets.

Common Mistakes

  • Measuring outside arm width instead of inside width.
  • Sending photos without any scale or dimension marks.
  • Forgetting that different buckets may have different ear spacing.
  • Assuming one regional excavator model always has identical pin data.

What Information to Send for Quote

  • Excavator brand and model
  • Operating weight
  • Pin diameter
  • Arm width
  • Center distance
  • Bucket ear width and photos

FAQ About This Topic
Can KRATOR match a coupler from photos?

Photos are useful, but pin diameter, arm width and center distance should still be confirmed for accurate manufacturing.

What if I do not know the pin size?

Send excavator model, machine plate and photos. KRATOR can guide you on which points to measure.

Can one coupler fit multiple buckets?

Yes, if the bucket pin dimensions and ear spacing are compatible or adapters are prepared.

Do you support custom brackets?

Yes. OEM bracket and pin size matching are available based on drawings or measurements.
