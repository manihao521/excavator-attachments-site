# KRATOR Competitor Intelligence

## Scope And Ethics

Use only publicly accessible information. Never access private data, bypass controls, or claim competitor traffic, rankings, sales, revenue, customers, or performance without a reliable cited source.

Do not copy competitor wording, descriptions, tables, images, FAQs, visual layouts, or page structures. Convert observations into original KRATOR opportunities grounded in KRATOR products, evidence, and buyer needs.

Default competitor groups may include Epiroc, Rammer, Indeco, Furukawa, and Soosan. Add another manufacturer or supplier only when it is relevant to the requested product, market, or buyer.

## Prepare

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/seo-strategy.md`, `docs/geo-ai-optimization.md`, and the relevant KRATOR source pages.
2. Inventory existing KRATOR product, category, blog, application, About, Factory, and Contact URLs before recommending a new page.
3. Define the product category, market, buyer, comparison dimensions, competitor set, and research date.
4. Confirm internet access. If unavailable, produce a research plan and state prominently that live competitor verification is incomplete.

## Evidence Labels

Label every material statement:

- **Verified fact:** Directly supported by a cited public URL and observed on the research date.
- **Observation:** A reproducible interpretation of visible page content or navigation.
- **Hypothesis:** A testable explanation or opportunity that needs more data.
- **Recommendation:** An original KRATOR action derived from facts and observations.

Do not turn a hypothesis into a fact. Record source URL, page title, competitor, access date, and the specific element reviewed.

## Research Workflow

1. Select representative official pages for each competitor: category, product, technical data, application, service, dealer, factory/company, contact, and resource content.
2. Capture evidence for product coverage, taxonomy, specification depth, guides, comparisons, maintenance, applications, OEM messaging, dealer messaging, trust proof, internal links, schema, FAQs, freshness, and conversion paths.
3. Review WhatsApp, quote, contact, dealer, and distributor paths without submitting forms.
4. Compare GEO and entity signals: organization identity, author/publisher clarity, product entities, About and factory proof, answer-ready passages, and citation-worthy technical material.
5. Compare every proposed opportunity with the current KRATOR repository. Mark it improve-existing, proposed, merge, or do-not-create.
6. Check keyword and intent overlap before proposing a URL. Route cluster expansion to `09-content-cluster-builder` when appropriate.
7. Score actions and state evidence requirements before implementation.

## Comparison Dimensions

- Product and subtype coverage
- Category and URL structure
- Product-page depth and technical specifications
- Buying guides, comparisons, maintenance, troubleshooting, and applications
- OEM, private-label, dealer, and distributor messaging
- Factory, quality, inspection, and company trust signals
- Internal-link patterns and content freshness
- Structured data and entity relationships
- FAQ coverage and visible-content parity
- Quote, WhatsApp, contact, and conversion paths
- GEO and AI citation readiness

## Priority Method

Score 1 to 5 with evidence:

- Inquiry potential: 30%
- Commercial relevance: 25%
- Topical authority: 20%
- Ease of implementation: 15%
- GEO value: 10%

Do not estimate traffic or ranking difficulty as fact without an approved data source.
