How to Match the Right Cucharón de excavadora

KRATOR excavator buckets are used for digging, trenching, rock loading, grading, screening and material handling. The right bucket should not be selected by excavator tonnage alone. Buyers should confirm excavator operating weight, bucket type, bucket width, bucket capacity, material density, tooth type, side cutter requirement, pin diameter, arm width, center distance and working condition before quotation.

BUYER FAQ

Cucharón de excavadora FAQ

How do I choose an excavator bucket?

Start with excavator operating weight, then confirm bucket type, bucket width, capacity, material density, tooth type and pin dimensions.

What information is needed before quotation?

Please send excavator brand, model, operating weight, bucket type, bucket width, pin diameter, arm width, center distance, material type, quantity and destination.

What is the difference between standard bucket, rock bucket and skeleton bucket?

A standard bucket is used for general digging, a rock bucket uses reinforced wear parts for abrasive material, and a skeleton bucket separates soil from rocks or demolition material.

Can KRATOR customize bucket width and capacity?

Yes. Bucket width, capacity, side cutters, teeth, cutting edge and wear plates can be discussed according to excavator size and material condition.

What measurements are needed for bucket fitment?

The key fitment measurements are pin diameter, arm width, center distance and coupler or linkage dimensions if a quick coupler is used.

Can you supply teeth, side cutters and cutting edges?

KRATOR can supply bucket teeth, side cutters, bolt-on edges, cutting edges and other wear parts according to bucket configuration.

Can you make buckets for different excavator brands?

Yes. Buckets can be matched for common excavator brands when the machine model and pin dimensions are confirmed.

How fast can I get a quotation?

If bucket type, excavator model and fitment dimensions are clear, KRATOR can provide a recommendation and quotation quickly.
