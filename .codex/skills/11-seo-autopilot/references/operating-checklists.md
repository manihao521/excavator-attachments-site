# KRATOR SEO Autopilot Operating Checklists

## 1. Weekly SEO Review

- Confirm review scope: source, current `dist/`, approved fresh build, preview, or live site.
- Record build execution status and timestamp; do not imply a stale `dist/` is current.
- Run website and structured-data audits.
- Check broken internal links, canonical, robots/noindex, sitemap, title, description, H1, and locale policy.
- Check image dimensions, large-file regressions, missing assets, and likely LCP priority.
- Review duplicate title/description groups and likely thin or overlapping pages.
- Review blog-to-product and product-to-guide links.
- Identify one to three evidence-backed content priorities.
- Flag pages with outdated dates, unsupported claims, incomplete specifications, or missing proof for human review.
- Review product quote path, WhatsApp, contact form, and dead ends.
- Return Hold / Ready for approved build / Ready for deployment approval. Never deploy automatically.

## 2. Monthly Growth Plan

- Review the previous month, available GSC data, qualified inquiry themes, and unfinished approved work.
- Run a repository overlap check and content-cluster plan.
- Recommend one pillar or buying guide, two to four support pages, one product improvement, and one comparison.
- Add one real factory, trust, or case-study opportunity only when evidence can be supplied.
- Add internal-link, GEO, competitor-gap, inquiry relevance, and factual-material requirements.
- Limit the plan to work that can be reviewed and shipped with quality in one month.

## 3. Quarterly Authority Audit

- Verify Organization, Brand, About, Factory, author, publisher, Product, and article relationships.
- Review product family, subtype, application, guide, comparison, and maintenance coverage.
- Review real factory, inspection, packing, document, and case-study evidence.
- Evaluate author responsibility, publication dates, citations, and technical review needs.
- Review brand mentions and ethical external authority opportunities.
- Test representative answer-engine queries and record date, location, language, cited source, and landing page.
- Identify consolidation, refresh, prune, redirect, and do-not-create decisions.

## 4. New Product Launch Workflow

1. Collect approved engineering, fitment, application, image, proof, and quote inputs.
2. Confirm taxonomy, canonical route, parent category, overlap, and generator ownership.
3. Build the product brief and page only after approval.
4. Plan product, guide, application, About/Factory, and Contact links.
5. Apply allowed structured data from visible facts only.
6. Optimize images without changing the product's appearance.
7. Run build, source/dist audits, preview, CTA, mobile, and asset checks.
8. Request separate publishing, deployment, and push approval.

## 5. Blog Publishing Workflow

1. Confirm cluster role, intent owner, target buyer, canonical URL, and cannibalization check.
2. Prepare a brief, then obtain product/factory factual review.
3. Draft original content with product links, related guides, proof, FAQ if useful, and qualified CTA.
4. Review title, description, H1, headings, images, dates, author/publisher, and schema eligibility.
5. Build and preview only after file changes are approved.
6. Publish, deploy, or push only under explicit separate instruction.

## 6. Ranking Decline Investigation

- Require affected query/page, country, device, date window, comparison window, and GSC exports or screenshots.
- Check indexing, canonical, robots, sitemap, status, template, content, links, schema, performance, and tracking changes.
- Separate confirmed causes from algorithm, competitor, seasonality, demand, and SERP-layout hypotheses.
- Recommend the smallest reversible test and measurement window.

## 7. GEO / AI Search Audit

- Verify entity consistency and stable internal relationships.
- Check whether main buyer questions have concise extractable answers.
- Check technical tables, definitions, comparisons, limitations, proof, dates, and author responsibility.
- Record representative AI/search results as observations, not guaranteed rankings.
- Stop schema work when valid markup already represents visible content; improve evidence or answers instead.

## 8. Technical SEO Regression Check

- Fresh build status
- Source-to-dist route parity
- Broken references and redirects
- Titles, descriptions, H1, canonical, robots, hreflang, sitemap
- JSON-LD syntax, duplicates, visible parity, prohibited fabricated fields
- Image existence, MIME type, dimensions, payload, loading priority
- Forms, WhatsApp, local-only URLs, mobile overflow

## 9. Conversion Improvement Review

- Map query -> understanding -> fit -> proof -> inquiry.
- Review CTA clarity, form qualification, WhatsApp continuity, mobile friction, and confirmation behavior.
- Prioritize by lead quality, buyer impact, confidence, effort, and measurable event.
- Avoid fake urgency, scarcity, testimonials, or manufacturer proof.

## 10. Multilingual Readiness Review

- Confirm approved English source and terminology.
- Check completeness, industrial terminology, units, model names, metadata, ALT, CTA, visible FAQ, and schema parity.
- Preserve locale `noindex`, sitemap exclusion, canonical, and hreflang until strategy change is approved.
- Verify Arabic RTL and mixed Latin model/number rendering.
