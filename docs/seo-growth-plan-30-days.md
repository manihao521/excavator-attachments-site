## 4. Competitor Gap Findings

Only public pages were reviewed. The observations below do not claim competitor traffic, rankings, sales, customers, or performance.

### Verified observations

- [Epiroc hydraulic breakers](https://www.epiroc.com/en-us/products/excavator-attachments/hydraulic-breaker) segments breaker families by carrier and application and publishes selection, maintenance, underwater-use, storage, working-tool, hose, lubricant, and service-kit information.
- Epiroc's public [working tools service card](https://www.epiroc.com/content/dam/epiroc/hydraulic-attachment-tools/service-cards/Epiroc_Service_Working_Tools_9864000501.pdf) maps tool forms such as moil points, chisels, and blunt tools to material and application conditions.
- [Rammer tools](https://www.rammer.com/en/aftermarket/tools/) exposes a large tool catalogue including standard, hard-rock, limestone, long-chisel, moil, pyramid, and compacting options. Its navigation also separates products, tools, parts, accessories, dealer locator, and distributor portal.
- [Rammer parts](https://www.rammer.com/en/aftermarket/parts/) provides a distinct aftermarket path, including tool-grease products.
- [Indeco's maintenance guide](https://indeco.it/en/2025/12/16/hammer-maintenance-complete-guide-to-lubrication-and-correct-use-of-the-indeco-hammer/) gives visible operational guidance around lubrication, tool care, positioning, stop conditions, common mistakes, and safety.
- [Furukawa hydraulic breakers](https://www.furukawarockdrill.co.jp/en/breaker) publishes model tables with oil flow, operating pressure, impact rate, rod diameter, and carrier-weight fields. Its [support area](https://www.furukawarockdrill.co.jp/en/support) separates manuals, maintenance, parts information, training, service, and overhaul support.
- A public [Soosan SB-series manual and parts book](https://www.soosanmachinery.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Manual-Parts-Book_Breaker_SB_Series.pdf) combines operation, inspection/maintenance, and parts information. This is evidence of the content pattern only; it is not evidence that KRATOR follows the same procedures.

### Inferences

- Technical support content is likely important during dealer evaluation because multiple established manufacturers expose tools, parts, maintenance, and support as first-class navigation or resources.
- Structured diagnostic and tool-selection information is well suited to search snippets and AI answers because it can resolve specific, bounded buyer questions.
- KRATOR can differentiate as a responsive B2B supplier by making the information required for model checking, diagnosis, spares, and quotation explicit, but only when backed by its own engineering and service process.

### Original KRATOR opportunities

- Publish an original symptom-led troubleshooting guide that tells users what to observe, what safe non-invasive checks to perform, when to stop, and what evidence to send KRATOR.
- Publish a KRATOR-specific chisel and working-tool selection guide based on the actual tool range, material, hardness, abrasiveness, carrier, and job.
- Explain spare-parts identification and dealer stocking without copying competitor catalogues or claiming unverified interchangeability.
- Connect application choice, routine maintenance, troubleshooting, product matching, quality evidence, and inquiry preparation as one two-way cluster.
- Build dealer-support authority by documenting the real support workflow rather than asserting generic global-service claims.
