RESPUESTA RÁPIDA

How to Match the Right Cizalla hidráulica

KRATOR hydraulic shears are excavator-mounted cutting attachments used for steel cutting, scrap recycling, demolition, structural dismantling and metal processing. The correct shear model should not be selected by excavator tonnage alone. Buyers should also confirm excavator operating weight, hydraulic oil flow, operating pressure, material type, steel thickness, cutting frequency, rotating or fixed type, jaw opening, blade length and working condition before quotation. For tool selection, compare cutting and crushing applications in the hydraulic pulverizer vs hydraulic shear guide and the demolition attachment guide.

Cómo elegir the Right Cizalla hidráulica Modelo

Select the hydraulic shear by excavator weight, hydraulic output, material type and cutting duty. Steel thickness, jaw opening and rotating or fixed type matter as much as carrier tonnage.

BUYER CHECK

Errores comunes When Buying a Cizalla hidráulica

  • Choosing only by excavator tonnage.
  • Not checking oil flow and pressure.
  • Ignoring steel thickness.
  • Choosing the wrong jaw opening.
  • Not confirming rotating or fixed type.
  • Using a small shear for heavy steel cutting.
  • Not preparing spare blades or wear parts.
  • Not checking working frequency and duty cycle.

BUYER FAQ

Cizalla hidráulica FAQ

How do I choose a hydraulic shear for my excavator?

Start with excavator operating weight, then confirm oil flow, operating pressure, material type, steel thickness, jaw opening and cutting frequency. KRATOR can recommend a suitable model after checking these details.

What information is needed before quotation?

Please send excavator brand, model, operating weight, hydraulic flow if available, pressure if available, material type, steel thickness, working condition, quantity and destination country or port.

What is the difference between rotating and fixed hydraulic shear?

A rotating shear helps adjust cutting direction on demolition sites, while a fixed shear is simpler and suitable for specific straight cutting or recycling work.

Can KRATOR customize jaw opening or bracket size?

Yes. Jaw opening, bracket size, pin dimensions and hose configuration can be discussed according to your excavator model and material type.

Can it cut steel beams, rebar and scrap metal?

Yes. Modelo selection depends on steel thickness, material shape, cutting frequency and excavator hydraulic capacity.

What excavator information should I provide?

Provide excavator model, operating weight, hydraulic flow, operating pressure if available, arm photos and current attachment information.

Do you supply blades and spare parts?

KRATOR can supply matching blades, wear parts, hoses and spare parts according to the selected model.

How fast can I get a quotation?

If machine and material information is clear, KRATOR can provide a model recommendation and quotation quickly.
