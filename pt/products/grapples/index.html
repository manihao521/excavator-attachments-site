How to Match the Right Garra hidráulica

KRATOR hydraulic grapples are excavator-mounted material handling attachments used for scrap handling, demolition sorting, log handling, waste recycling, stone handling and material loading. The correct grapple model should not be selected by excavator tonnage alone. Buyers should also confirm excavator operating weight, hydraulic oil flow, operating pressure, material type, material size, grapple type, tine or claw quantity, opening width, rotating or fixed type and working condition before quotation.

BUYER FAQ

Garra hidráulica FAQ

How do I choose a hydraulic grapple for my excavator?

Start with excavator operating weight, then confirm hydraulic flow, pressure, material type, material size, opening width, claw quantity and whether you need a rotating or fixed type.

What information is needed before quotation?

Please send excavator brand, model, operating weight, material type, material size, working condition, grapple type, quantity and destination country or port.

What is the difference between sorting grapple, log grapple and orange peel grapple?

Sorting grapples are used for demolition and mixed materials, log grapples are used for timber handling, and orange peel grapples are often used for scrap, waste and bulk material loading.

Can KRATOR customize opening width or bracket size?

Yes. Opening width, bracket size, pin dimensions, hose configuration and grapple type can be discussed according to your excavator and material handling project.

Can it handle scrap metal, logs, waste and demolition materials?

Yes, but model selection depends on material type, density, size, operating frequency and excavator hydraulic capacity.

What excavator information should I provide?

Provide excavator model, operating weight, hydraulic flow, operating pressure if available, arm photos and current attachment information.

Do you supply pins, hoses and spare parts?

KRATOR can supply matching pins, hoses, wear parts and spare parts according to the selected hydraulic grapple model.

How fast can I get a quotation?

If machine and material information is clear, KRATOR can provide a model recommendation and quotation quickly.
