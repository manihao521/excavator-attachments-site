How to Match the Right كسارة خرسانة هيدروليكية

KRATOR hydraulic pulverizers are excavator-mounted concrete crushing attachments used for concrete demolition, secondary demolition, rebar separation, building demolition and recycling work. The correct pulverizer model should not be selected by excavator tonnage alone. Buyers should also confirm excavator operating weight, hydraulic oil flow, operating pressure, concrete thickness, rebar condition, jaw opening, crushing force, rotating or fixed type and working condition before quotation. For a detailed model checklist, read the concrete pulverizer buying guide and the hydraulic pulverizer vs hydraulic shear comparison.

BUYER MATCHING TIPS

BUYER FAQ

كسارة خرسانة هيدروليكية FAQ

How do I choose a hydraulic pulverizer for my excavator?

Start with excavator operating weight, then confirm oil flow, operating pressure, concrete thickness, rebar condition, jaw opening, crushing force, rotating or fixed type and jobsite duty.

What information is needed before quotation?

Please send excavator brand, model, operating weight, hydraulic flow if available, pressure if available, concrete thickness, rebar condition, working condition, quantity and destination country or port.

What is the difference between hydraulic pulverizer and hydraulic shear?

A hydraulic pulverizer is mainly used for crushing concrete and separating rebar, while a hydraulic shear is mainly used for cutting steel structures, scrap metal and beams.

Can KRATOR customize jaw opening or bracket size?

Yes. Jaw opening, bracket size, pin dimensions and hose configuration can be discussed according to your excavator model and concrete condition.

Can it crush reinforced concrete and separate rebar?

Yes. الموديل selection depends on concrete thickness, rebar condition, crushing frequency and excavator hydraulic capacity.

What excavator information should I provide?

Provide excavator model, operating weight, hydraulic flow, operating pressure if available, arm photos and current attachment information.

Do you supply teeth, blades and spare parts?

KRATOR can supply matching teeth, blades, wear parts, hoses and spare parts according to the selected model.

How fast can I get a quotation?

If machine and concrete information is clear, KRATOR can provide a model recommendation and quotation quickly.
