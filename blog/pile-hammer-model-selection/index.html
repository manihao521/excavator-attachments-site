How to Select an Excavator Mounted Pile Hammer

Pile hammer selection depends on excavator class, pile type, pile length, soil condition, clamp structure and required centrifugal force.

7 min readKRATOR Technical TeamUpdated 2026
On This Page

Quick Answer

A pile hammer should be selected by excavator operating weight, pile profile, pile length, soil condition and required centrifugal force. The clamp type must match the pile material and shape.

Key Selection Factors

Excavator weight

Carrier weight determines the hammer size and hydraulic requirement.

Pile profile

Sheet pile, pipe pile and H-beam profiles need different clamps.

Pile length

Longer piles usually need stronger vibration and better clamping force.

Soil condition

Soft soil, sand, clay and mixed ground require different driving energy.

Clamp type

Side grip and top clamp styles serve different site access conditions.

Hydraulic flow

Stable flow and pressure are required for consistent vibration.

Why Excavator weight Matters

Carrier weight determines the hammer size and hydraulic requirement. This point should be confirmed before production so the attachment can be matched to the real carrier and jobsite.

How to Check Pile profile

Sheet pile, pipe pile and H-beam profiles need different clamps. Clear photos, machine information and simple notes make the matching process faster for overseas buyers.

Final Check Before Ordering

Confirm excavator model, operating weight, attachment configuration, hydraulic requirement, dimensions if available, quantity and destination before placing the order.

Common Mistakes

  • Choosing by price or product name without checking excavator data.
  • Not sending photos, dimensions or hydraulic information when they are needed.
  • Ignoring jobsite material, working condition and duty cycle.
  • Forgetting spare parts, bracket matching and shipping details.

What Information to Send for Quote

  • Excavator brand and model
  • Operating weight
  • Attachment type
  • Working condition
  • Photos or drawings
  • Quantity and destination
