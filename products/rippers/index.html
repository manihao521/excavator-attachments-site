BUYER FAQ

Excavator Ripper FAQ

How do I choose an excavator ripper?

Start with excavator operating weight, then confirm ground condition, rock hardness, ripping depth, tooth type and bracket dimensions.

What information is needed before quotation?

Please send excavator brand, model, operating weight, ground condition, required ripping depth, pin diameter, arm width, center distance, quantity and destination.

What is the difference between single shank and multi shank ripper?

A single shank ripper is used for concentrated ripping force in hard ground, while multi shank options can cover wider loosening areas in lighter ground conditions.

Can KRATOR customize the ripper bracket?

Yes. Bracket dimensions, pin size, arm width and center distance can be matched according to your excavator or quick coupler.

Can a ripper replace a hydraulic breaker?

A ripper loosens hard ground or rock before digging or breaking. A hydraulic breaker is still needed when impact breaking is required.

Do you supply spare teeth and wear parts?

KRATOR can supply replaceable ripper teeth and related wear parts according to the selected ripper model.

Can you make rippers for different excavator brands?

Yes. Rippers can be matched for common excavator brands when the excavator model and pin dimensions are provided.

How fast can I get a quotation?

If machine data and ground condition are clear, KRATOR can provide a model recommendation and quotation quickly.
