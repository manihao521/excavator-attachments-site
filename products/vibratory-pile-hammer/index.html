How to Choose the Right Vibratory Pile Hammer Model

Select the pile hammer by excavator hydraulic capacity, pile profile, clamp requirement and jobsite condition. Heavy sheet pile projects need more than a simple carrier tonnage check.

Key selection factors

  • Excavator operating weight
  • Hydraulic oil flow and operating pressure
  • Pile material, pile size and required driving depth
  • Soil condition and continuous working time
  • Side grip or top clamp requirement
  • Jaw opening, clamp force and jobsite space

BUYER FAQ

Vibratory Pile Hammer FAQ

How do I choose a vibratory pile hammer for my excavator?

Start with excavator operating weight, oil flow and pressure, then confirm pile type, pile size, soil condition and clamp requirement.

What information is needed before quotation?

Please send excavator brand and model, operating weight, oil flow if available, operating pressure if available, pile type, pile size, soil condition, working depth and destination country or port.

What is the difference between side grip and top clamp pile hammer?

Side grip designs help handle piles in limited headroom and jobsite spaces, while top clamp designs are often used for more vertical driving work and specific pile profiles.

Can KRATOR customize the clamp or jaw size?

Yes. Jaw opening, clamp type and bracket configuration can be discussed according to pile profile and excavator data.

Can it drive sheet piles, solar piles and guardrail posts?

Yes, model selection depends on pile profile, pile size, soil condition and excavator hydraulic capacity.

What excavator information should I provide?

Provide excavator model, operating weight, hydraulic flow, operating pressure if available, arm photos and current attachment information.

Do you supply hoses, clamps and spare parts?

KRATOR can supply matching hoses, clamp options, jaw parts and spare parts based on the selected model.

How fast can I get a quotation?

If machine and pile information is clear, KRATOR can provide a model recommendation and quotation quickly.
