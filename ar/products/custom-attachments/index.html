What Custom Excavator Attachments Can KRATOR Make?

KRATOR can develop custom excavator attachments according to excavator model, jobsite condition, attachment function, mounting dimensions and hydraulic requirements. Custom projects may include mounting brackets, special buckets, custom rippers, custom grapples, special demolition tools, piling tool modifications, hydraulic attachment adaptations, OEM color and logo support, and dealer batch customization. A custom attachment quotation should not be based only on excavator tonnage. Buyers should provide working condition, drawings, photos, pin dimensions and hydraulic data.

BUYER CHECK

أخطاء شائعة in Custom Attachment Projects

  • Sending only excavator tonnage
  • Not confirming pin diameter
  • Ignoring arm width and center distance
  • Not explaining the working condition
  • Not providing photos or drawings
  • Ignoring hydraulic flow requirements
  • Choosing weak structure for heavy-duty work
  • Not confirming quantity and packing requirements

FAQ

ملحقات مخصصة FAQ

Common questions about OEM, ODM and project-based excavator attachment customization.

Can KRATOR make custom excavator attachments?

Yes. KRATOR can evaluate custom mounting, structure, hydraulic configuration and project-based attachment requirements.

What information is needed for a custom attachment quotation?

Please send excavator model, operating weight, working condition, attachment function, pin dimensions, hydraulic data, drawings or photos, quantity and destination.

Can you customize mounting brackets?

KRATOR can support pin-on brackets, quick coupler matching and special bracket structures according to machine dimensions.

Can you make OEM color and logo for distributors?

OEM color, logo, labels and packing support can be provided for dealer and batch order requirements.

Can you customize hydraulic attachments?

Hydraulic attachment projects can be evaluated by oil flow, operating pressure, hose configuration and control requirements.

Do I need drawings before asking for a quote?

Drawings help speed up evaluation, but clear photos, dimensions and working condition details can also be used for initial matching.

Can you help match pin dimensions?

Yes. Please provide pin diameter, arm width, center distance and photos of the excavator arm or existing attachment.

How fast can I get a custom quotation?

KRATOR usually replies within 12 hours after receiving sufficient project and machine information.
