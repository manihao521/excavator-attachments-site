FAQ & الموارد

Find answers to common questions and explore expert articles to help you choose the right attachment.

How do I choose the right attachment for my excavator?

Consider your excavator model, operating weight, hydraulic flow and pressure, and the type of work. Our team can help you match the right attachment for maximum performance and efficiency.

What information is needed for a quote?

Please send your excavator brand, model, operating weight, working condition, required attachment type, and photos if available.

Can you customize bracket and pin size?

Yes. We can customize brackets, pin size and mounting dimensions according to your excavator model or drawings.

What is the delivery time?

Delivery time depends on product type, order quantity and customization requirements. We will confirm the estimated lead time before order confirmation.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. We support global shipping by sea, air or express depending on order size and delivery requirements.
