How to Match the Right Hydraulic Breaker

KRATOR hydraulic breakers are excavator-mounted rock breaker attachments used for concrete demolition, rock breaking, road construction, quarry work, trenching and foundation jobs. The correct breaker model should not be selected by excavator tonnage alone. For stable performance, buyers should also confirm oil flow, operating pressure, pin diameter, arm width, center distance and the real working condition before quotation. For demolition jobs, compare breaker, pulverizer and shear options in the demolition attachment guide.

MODEL SELECTION

How to Choose the Right Hydraulic Breaker Model

A hydraulic breaker must match the excavator carrier, hydraulic output and jobsite material. Confirm the excavator operating weight, oil flow, operating pressure, rod or chisel diameter, application, bracket dimensions and working environment before final model selection.

Key selection factors

  • Excavator operating weight and carrier class
  • Hydraulic oil flow and operating pressure range
  • Rod or chisel diameter required for the work
  • Concrete, road, quarry, trenching or mining application
  • Pin diameter, arm width and center distance for bracket matching
  • Dust, hard rock, continuous duty cycle and working environment

BUYING CHECK

Common Mistakes When Buying a Hydraulic Breaker

  • Choosing only by excavator tonnage without checking hydraulic flow.
  • Ignoring operating pressure requirements for the breaker model.
  • Not checking pin diameter, arm width and center distance before bracket production.
  • Choosing the wrong chisel type for concrete, trenching or rock work.
  • Using a breaker that is too small for hard rock or continuous quarry duty.
  • Not preparing spare parts such as seal kits, bushings and chisels.

BUYER FAQ

Hydraulic Breaker FAQ

How do I choose a hydraulic breaker for my excavator?

Start with excavator operating weight, then confirm oil flow, operating pressure, bracket dimensions and the material you need to break. KRATOR can recommend a suitable model after checking these details.

What information do you need before quotation?

Please send excavator brand, model, operating weight, pin diameter, arm width, center distance, working condition, quantity and destination country or port.

Can KRATOR customize the bracket and pin size?

Yes. KRATOR can customize the bracket, pin diameter, arm width and mounting dimensions according to your excavator model or drawings.

What chisel types are available?

Common choices include moil point, wedge, flat and blunt chisels. The right type depends on concrete demolition, trenching, road repair, quarry or rock breaking work.

Can the breaker fit CAT, Komatsu, Hitachi, Volvo, Doosan and Hyundai excavators?

Yes. KRATOR supports model-matched bracket production for major excavator brands when machine model and mounting dimensions are confirmed.

Do you supply spare parts?

Yes. Spare parts such as chisels, seal kits, bushings, diaphragms and related wear parts can be supplied according to the breaker model.

How is the hydraulic breaker packed for export?

Hydraulic breakers are packed with export-ready protection according to order size, shipping method and destination requirements.

How fast can I get a quotation?

If the excavator model, tonnage, bracket dimensions and working condition are clear, KRATOR can usually provide a model recommendation and quotation quickly.
