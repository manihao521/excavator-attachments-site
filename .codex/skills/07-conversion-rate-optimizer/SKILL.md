# KRATOR Conversion Rate Optimizer

## Analyze

Map the path from landing query to product understanding, fit confirmation, trust, and inquiry. Review desktop and mobile separately.

Check:

- Primary and secondary CTA visibility without obstructing content.
- WhatsApp links, prefilled messages, email links, and form submission behavior.
- Quote fields needed for fit and pricing: machine model, weight, attachment, hydraulic data, pin dimensions, work condition, quantity, and destination.
- Product proof: specifications, applications, factory or inspection evidence, packing support, and clear limits.
- Friction: unclear labels, duplicate choices, long forms, dead ends, weak confirmation states, or mobile overlap.
- Continuity between product pages, technical guides, matching support, and contact pages.

## Recommend

Prioritize changes by buyer impact, confidence, implementation effort, and measurement plan. Prefer practical B2B clarity over urgency tricks, popups, fake scarcity, or inflated claims.

For each recommendation, state the observed problem, target buyer, proposed change, expected behavior, event or lead metric, and guardrail metric. Do not change the page during an audit-only request.
