How to Match the Right Excavator Quick Coupler

KRATOR quick couplers help excavator operators change buckets, breakers, grapples, rippers, compactors and other attachments faster. The correct quick coupler should not be selected by excavator tonnage alone. Buyers should confirm pin diameter, arm width, center distance, bucket ear width, hydraulic line availability, working condition and the attachment types that will be used before quotation. If the coupler will be used with demolition tools, the demolition attachment guide helps compare attachment combinations.

BUYER MATCHING TIPS

Buyer Matching Tips

  • Confirm pin diameter, arm width and center distance.
  • Check if the coupler is for pin-on attachments or existing tools.
  • Confirm excavator tonnage and attachment weight.
  • Check safety lock type.
  • Send clear photos of the excavator arm and current bucket linkage.

How to Choose the Right Quick Coupler Model

A quick coupler must match the excavator linkage, attachment plan and safety requirement. Confirm excavator operating weight, pin diameter, arm width, center distance, attachment type, hydraulic line availability, coupler type and working environment before final selection.

Key selection factors

  • Excavator operating weight and carrier class
  • Pin diameter, arm width and center distance
  • Bucket ear width and existing attachment dimensions
  • Bucket, breaker, grapple, ripper or compactor use
  • Mechanical, hydraulic or tilt quick coupler requirement
  • Hydraulic line availability, safety lock and working environment

BUYER FAQ

Quick Coupler FAQ

How do I choose a quick coupler for my excavator?

Start with excavator operating weight, then confirm pin diameter, arm width, center distance, bucket ear width, attachment type and whether hydraulic lines are available.

What measurements are needed before quotation?

Please send pin diameter, arm width, center distance, bucket ear width if available, excavator model and photos or drawings of the linkage.

What is the difference between hydraulic and mechanical quick coupler?

A hydraulic quick coupler is operated from the cab when hydraulic lines are available. A mechanical quick coupler has a simpler locking method and is often used for lower frequency attachment changes.

Can KRATOR customize pin diameter and arm width?

Yes. KRATOR can match pin diameter, arm width, center distance and bracket configuration according to your excavator and attachments.

Can one quick coupler fit buckets, breakers and grapples?

It can be planned for multiple attachments when pin dimensions and attachment ear widths are compatible. KRATOR will check the attachment list before recommendation.

Do I need hydraulic lines for a hydraulic quick coupler?

Yes, hydraulic operation requires suitable hydraulic lines and control. If hydraulic lines are not available, a mechanical coupler may be considered.

What safety lock options are available?

Safety lock options depend on coupler type and excavator configuration. KRATOR can recommend a suitable locking design based on working condition.

How fast can I get a quotation?

If the excavator model, pin dimensions, attachment list and destination are clear, KRATOR can usually provide a quick model recommendation and quotation.
