BUYER QUESTIONS

Application Selection FAQ

Short answers for choosing excavator attachments by working condition.

Which attachment is best for demolition work?

Hydraulic breakers, shears and pulverizers are commonly used depending on concrete, steel and recycling requirements.

What information is needed for attachment recommendation?

Please send excavator brand, model, tonnage, working condition and photos if available.

Can KRATOR match bracket and pin size?

Yes. Bracket, pin diameter, arm width and center distance can be confirmed before production.

Do you support custom attachments for special jobsites?

Yes. OEM / ODM and custom mounting solutions are available.
