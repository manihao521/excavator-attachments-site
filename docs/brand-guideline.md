# KRATOR ATTACHMENTS Brand Guideline

## Brand Foundation

KRATOR ATTACHMENTS is an excavator attachment manufacturer and B2B export supplier serving distributors, equipment dealers, rental fleets, contractors, demolition companies, and procurement teams.

The brand promise is practical: help a buyer identify, match, confirm, and order the right attachment with clear technical and export information.

## Positioning

KRATOR should feel:

- Industrial and technically capable
- Premium but not decorative
- Direct, responsive, and export-ready
- Specific about matching and order requirements
- Honest about what is verified, available, or still needs confirmation

KRATOR should not feel like a startup SaaS product, a consumer hardware store, a generic marketplace, or an AI-generated content site.

## Audience Priorities

1. Distributors and dealers need repeatable supply, OEM support, technical data, and fast quotation help.
2. Contractors need correct machine fit, application suitability, durability, and delivery clarity.
3. Rental fleets need compatibility, serviceability, wear parts, and predictable repeat orders.
4. Procurement teams need documentation, packing details, commercial clarity, and risk reduction.

## Voice

Use clear international English. Sound like an experienced attachment supplier who asks the right questions.

- Prefer concrete nouns, measurements, and decision criteria.
- Explain tradeoffs without attacking competitors.
- Use qualified language when an item depends on machine data or order conditions.
- Keep CTAs direct: `Get Quote`, `WhatsApp Us`, `Send Machine Data`, `Check Compatibility`.
- Avoid empty phrases such as "world-class quality," "best solution," or "one-stop service" without proof.

## Visual System

The current CSS establishes the core palette:

| Role | Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary navy | `--navy` | `#052946` | Header, strong sections, technical depth |
| Deep navy | `--navy-2` | `#021A2F` | Footer, dark bands, overlays |
| Industrial background | `--ds-bg` | `#071A2C` | Main dark surfaces |
| Dark card | `--ds-card` | `#0B2740` | Repeated technical modules |
| Engineering yellow | `--yellow` | `#F7B500` | Primary CTA, key accents, active states |
| Yellow dark | `--yellow-dark` | `#D89000` | Hover and contrast support |
| Steel gray | `--color-steel` | `#AAB4C0` | Secondary text and technical labels |

| White | `--color-white` | `#FFFFFF` | High-contrast text and clean surfaces |

Use yellow as an accent, not as a page-wide background. Use gradients only when they support existing industrial depth; do not make them the visual identity.

## Typography

- Headings: Montserrat, Inter, Arial, sans-serif
- Body: Inter, Montserrat, Arial, sans-serif
- Use strong, compact headings and readable body text.
- Do not rely on extreme letter spacing, oversized hero text, or tiny uppercase labels for core information.
- Technical tables and form labels must remain readable on mobile.

## Imagery

Preferred imagery:

- Real attachment products shown clearly
- Factory fabrication, welding, machining, inspection, and painting
- Packing, spare parts, container loading, and export preparation
- Excavators in relevant demolition, piling, earthmoving, recycling, and utility work
- Dimension confirmation, bracket, pin, hose, and hydraulic details

Avoid:

- Generic construction stock images that do not show the product
- Dark, blurred, or over-cropped hero media
- Cartoon machinery or decorative 3D icons as the primary visual
- AI-looking factories, impossible hydraulics, unreadable embedded text, or invented logos
- Unverified certificates, badges, customer logos, or production claims

## Layout and Components

- Use full-width industrial bands and constrained content shells.
- Keep navigation, product cards, specification tables, FAQs, proof modules, and CTAs consistent with existing patterns.
- Cards should be restrained, repeatable, and no more rounded than the current system.
- Show the product or company subject clearly in the first viewport.
- Use familiar controls and visible labels; icon-only actions need accessible names.
- Keep the same content meaning on desktop and mobile.

## Trust Presentation

Trust must come from evidence, not decoration:

- Verified specification tables
- Machine-matching inputs and process
- Real factory, inspection, packing, and loading photos
- Clear company and contact information
- Honest document availability language
- Internal links to quality control, custom service, applications, and technical guides

## Release Checklist

- Brand name is exactly `KRATOR ATTACHMENTS` where the formal entity is intended.
- Navy/yellow/steel palette is consistent with existing tokens.
- Real industrial imagery is clear and correctly labeled.
- Claims are supported or explicitly qualified.
- Desktop and mobile layouts are complete and free of overlap.
- CTAs lead to working WhatsApp, email, or quote paths.
- No startup SaaS, cartoon, generic AI, or fake trust styling was introduced.
