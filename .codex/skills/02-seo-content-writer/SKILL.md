# KRATOR SEO Content Writer

## Prepare

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/brand-guideline.md`, `docs/seo-strategy.md`, and the relevant existing pages. Establish the primary query, buyer stage, target customer, product facts, proof assets, and conversion action.

Never invent specifications, certifications, factory capabilities, customer names, test results, delivery times, warranties, prices, or market claims. Mark missing facts as required inputs.

## Write

- Answer the search intent early in plain industrial English.
- Use concrete machine, hydraulic, mounting, application, material, and order details where verified.
- Keep each page distinct; do not spin near-duplicate product copy.
- Use descriptive headings, scannable paragraphs, real tables, and decision guidance.
- Add visible FAQs only when they solve real buyer questions.
- Suggest contextual links to relevant product, category, application, quality, contact, and blog pages.
- Keep the CTA tied to qualified data: excavator model, operating weight, pin dimensions, hydraulic flow and pressure, working condition, quantity, and destination.

## Deliver

Provide:

1. Search intent and page role.
2. SEO title and meta description.
3. H1 and heading outline.
4. Page copy or an approval-ready brief.
5. FAQ recommendations.
6. Internal-link source and destination suggestions.
7. Required proof, images, or facts still missing.

Apply the schema policy in `AGENTS.md`; writing an FAQ does not automatically authorize `FAQPage` markup.
